1Receive

A scene arrives: a push through the bridge, the answer to a pull, or a .pulley file.

background extension

2Plan

pulley-core checks the scene again and turns it into a plan with the panel's options. All the math happens here.

pulley-core (WebAssembly), aeplan.rs

3Write

The plan and one gradient preset per gradient go to Pulley's temp folder (faster than passing text to ExtendScript).

tmp/plan-<id>.txt, tmp/grad-<id>-<n>.ffx

4Build

One evalScript call builds every layer inside one undo group, reporting progress and checking for Cancel every 50 operations.

ExtendScript, apply.jsx

5Finish

Gradient colors are applied from the presets and your layer selection restored. The result goes to the panel and back to the sender.

temp files deleted
