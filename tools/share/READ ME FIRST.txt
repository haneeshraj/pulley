PULLEY 0.1.0 for Windows
Send artwork from Illustrator to After Effects as real, editable shape layers.
Created by Haneesh Raj Banisetti. MIT License (see LICENSE.txt).

WHAT YOU NEED
- Windows 10 or 11
- Illustrator 2025 and After Effects 2025 (other versions are untested)

INSTALL
1. Unzip this whole folder somewhere, for example your Desktop. (Don't run it from inside the zip.)
2. Close Illustrator and After Effects.
3. Double-click "Install Pulley.bat".
   - If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
     Pulley isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows doesn't recognize it.
4. The Pulley bridge installer opens. Click through it. A Pulley icon appears in the system tray
   (bottom-right, you may need to click the ^ arrow). That small app carries artwork between
   Illustrator and After Effects and only talks to your own computer.

USE IT
1. Open After Effects and a comp. Go to Window > Extensions > Pulley. The dot should turn green.
2. Open Illustrator and your file. Go to Window > Extensions > Pulley.
3. Select artwork in Illustrator and click "Push to After Effects".
4. It appears as layers in the active comp.

TIPS
- Every shape as its own layer: in the After Effects Pulley panel, click the gear icon, then under
  Layers choose "One layer per shape (break groups apart)".
- Push the same artwork again to update the layers instead of getting copies. Animation you added
  in After Effects is kept.
- Illustrator effects (drop shadows, glows, blurs) come in as picture layers under the shapes.
  Untick "Effects as pictures" in the Illustrator panel to skip them.

IF IT SAYS "NOT CONNECTED"
- Check the Pulley icon is in the system tray. If not, open "Pulley" from the Start menu.
- Click the circular arrow next to "Not connected" in the panel.
- Still stuck: quit and reopen Illustrator and After Effects.

UNINSTALL
- Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Pulley > Uninstall (removes the bridge).
- Delete the folders dev.pulley.illustrator and dev.pulley.ae from
  %APPDATA%\Adobe\CEP\extensions (paste that into File Explorer's address bar).
